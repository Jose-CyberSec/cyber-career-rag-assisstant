Source Title: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics - Computer Support Specialists
Source Type: Official occupational outlook summary
Source URL: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-support-specialists.htm
Date Collected: 2026-06-02

Content:
Computer Support Specialists provide technical help to users and organizations. They may troubleshoot computer problems, support hardware and software, maintain networks, assist users, and document issues through support processes.

For someone trying to enter cybersecurity, IT Support and Help Desk roles can be a strong stepping stone. These roles build practical experience with troubleshooting, customer support, Windows and software issues, basic networking, account access, ticketing systems, and technical documentation.

Skills commonly connected to support roles:
- Troubleshooting hardware and software
- User support
- Windows and operating system basics
- Networking fundamentals
- Password and account issues
- Printer, VPN, and email support
- Ticketing systems
- Customer communication
- Documentation
- Escalation procedures

Why this helps cybersecurity:
SOC and security analyst roles often require clear documentation, escalation, troubleshooting, and understanding user/system behavior. Help Desk experience can show that a candidate can solve technical problems, communicate professionally, and work with real users and systems.
